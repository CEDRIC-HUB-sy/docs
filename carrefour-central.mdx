## Introduction

Carrefour Central is a modern and dynamic community built to bring together players, creators, developers, and passionate individuals in a professional, active, and safe environment. The project stands out for its solid management, organization, and commitment to offering a space where members can collaborate, grow, and contribute to various community projects.

This document explains the structure, mission, values, and internal operations of Carrefour Central.

## General overview

Carrefour Central serves as a community hub, designed to centralize multiple projects, partnerships, and activities.

## Safety & rules

Safety and respect are top priorities. Carrefour Central enforces clear rules to keep the community healthy and enjoyable.

### Main rules

<Warning>
  All members must follow these rules to maintain a positive environment
</Warning>

- No toxicity, harassment, or disrespect
- No spam or unauthorized advertising
- Respect staff and other members
- No inappropriate or harmful content
- Follow channel purposes and bot command guidelines
- Follow mention/ping limitations

### Possible sanctions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Warnings">
    First offense typically results in a warning
  </Step>
  <Step title="Temporary mute">
    Repeated violations may lead to temporary mute
  </Step>
  <Step title="Temporary or permanent ban">
    Serious or continued violations result in ban
  </Step>
</Steps>
